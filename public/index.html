INSTANT VOCAL REMOVAL • STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Vocal Remover

Choose a stereo audio or supported video file. The centre vocal is removed band by band and the backing is rebuilt in seconds — nothing is uploaded and no model has to download.

Shape your karaoke backing

How hard the centre vocal is cut. 100% removes the most voice; lower values keep more of the original for a fuller feel. Works well on most pop and rock masters.

Keeps the opening untouched before the vocal removal fades in. 0 = off.

Choose a stereo song to begin.

Compare original & karaoke

Switch versions at the same position. Original is the uploaded file before key changes. Use headphones for the clearest comparison.

Karaoke WAV and recorder downloads share your daily allowance. 9 free downloads per day unless your account has Pro.

Everything runs in your browser: no upload, no model download, no account. The remover measures, for every frequency in the voice range, how much of it is centred (a lead vocal is dead centre, guitars and keys are not) and cuts only that centred part, leaving bass, cymbals and stereo width intact. A typical song takes a few seconds. Vocals that are heavily doubled, delayed or very wet can leave faint traces — that is the physical limit of a centre-based separation; nothing is sent anywhere to do better.
