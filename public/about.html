About SingVaza

Our Mission

Millions of talented everyday singers never share their voice simply because raw smartphone recordings sound dry, delayed, and thin compared to professionally mixed studio tracks—while traditional Digital Audio Workstations require expensive hardware and months of technical training.

SingVaza was built to close that gap right inside any mobile or desktop browser. With zero heavy software downloads and smooth performance even on entry-level Android devices, singers can rehearse lyrics, record takes, align latency, trim audio cleanly, apply studio-grade EQ, compression, and concert hall reverb, and export lightweight stereo MP3 files ready to share in seconds.

100% Local Browser Audio Privacy

Unlike cloud upload tools that send private voice recordings to remote servers and charge heavy bandwidth fees, SingVaza runs its entire vocal signal processing engine—noise gating, pitch analysis, SoundTouch key shifting, stereo hall convolution/feedback networks, and LAME MP3 encoding—100% locally on your own device using WebAudio and Web Workers.

Your raw voice recordings remain on your device unless you personally choose to share your exported MP3 or WAV file. Cloud storage on Cloudflare D1 stores only your account credentials and lightweight mix parameter presets so you can pick up your favorite vocal sound across devices.

Accessible Worldwide

SingVaza is supported by non-intrusive advertising so singers in every country can enjoy unlimited singing practice, free educational vocal guides, a 30-day complimentary Pro launch pass when signing in, and automatic daily free MP3 downloads resetting at midnight in each singer's own local timezone.
