Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

1. 100% Local Browser Microphone & Voice Processing

SingVaza requests access to your device microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO / Web MediaDevices) solely when you actively click Start Recording or Test Mic. All vocal recording, EQ, compression, pitch correction, reverb mixing, and MP3/WAV audio rendering run 100% locally inside your web browser memory. Your raw voice recordings are never uploaded to or stored on SingVaza servers.

2. Account Information, Google Sign-In & Local Timezone Quota

  • Account Data: When you sign up with email or Google Sign-In (Google Identity Services), we store your verified email address, display name, session token, 30-day complimentary Pro pass expiration timestamp, and saved mix parameter presets in an encrypted Cloudflare D1 database.
  • Global Local-Midnight Quota Reset: To reset your complimentary daily MP3/WAV download allowance accurately at 12:00 AM midnight in your country, our edge server reads standard regional timezone headers (such as Asia/Colombo, Europe/London, or America/New_York) without storing precise GPS coordinates.

3. Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense & Google AdMob Disclosure)

To keep SingVaza free for singers worldwide, we may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense and Google AdMob.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google Advertising / DoubleClick DART cookie or mobile advertising identifiers) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Third-Party Media & API Services

Embedded karaoke videos use official YouTube IFrame Embed playback governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Security Check & Terms Choice

SingVaza uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the studio and account sign-in from automated abuse. When you complete a check, Cloudflare may process information such as your IP address and browser details to evaluate the request. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

An essential, secure browser-session cookie records that the security check was completed (up to 12 hours). Your acceptance of the current Terms & Conditions is saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data removes this saved choice.

6. Permanent One-Click Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently and immediately delete your SingVaza account, sessions, and all cloud presets at any time directly inside the app: click your profile button in the top navigation bar and select Delete Account & Cloud Data, or reach us via our Contact Support page.
