Every week a rumour about Amitabh Bachchan's health does the rounds on WhatsApp, and every week it is the same rumour recycled with a new date on it. This time he answered it himself. If you are going to share one thing about him this month, share the interview — not the forwarded screenshot.

Photo: file

What is confirmed

  • He addressed the health and retirement rumours himself, in a public response this month, at the age of 84. That is from him, not from a "close friend of the family".
  • He is still working. His slate for the coming year has not been withdrawn, and no retirement announcement has been made by him or by his office.

What is rumour, and stays rumour

  • Anything described as a "hospital source". No hospital has confirmed anything, and a screenshot of a message is not a source.
  • Old photographs. Most of the "last photo" posts circulating are years old. Check the date on the picture before you share it.
  • Anything about money or family disputes. Nothing of that sort has been confirmed by anyone with a name.

One more thing worth sharing instead

He is reported to be in talks to rejoin Tamil cinema for Aishwarya Rajinikanth's Texla, which has Jai in the lead. That is a rumour — reported by media, not confirmed by the actor or the production. If it happens, it would be his second Tamil film after Vettaiyan, and the story would be worth a proper article. Until then, it stays in this section, which is where I put everything I cannot verify.

Why it matters

The internet has decided that a man of 84 is not allowed to be tired without a headline saying he is dying. I would rather write about his work than his health. But since the rumours have reached the point where people are messaging me asking whether he is alive, here is the straight answer: he is, he is working, and the only source for anything beyond that is Amitabh Bachchan.
