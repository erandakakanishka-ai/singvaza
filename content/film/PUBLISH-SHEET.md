## The four house rules these posts follow

1. **A named source for every fact.** No "sources say". If it is from a report, name the outlet and say it is not confirmed.
2. **Rumours stay in the rumour section.** Never in the headline, never as a fact.
3. **No spoilers before the ⚠️ warning,** and a score at the end of every review.
4. **Only legal places to watch.** Cinema, Netflix, Prime Video, Dialog ViU, Peo TV — never a torrent, a Telegram channel or a "free HD" site. The admin panel blocks those links anyway.
