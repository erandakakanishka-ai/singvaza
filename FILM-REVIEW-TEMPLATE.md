# SingVaza blog — film writing (English)

Two kinds of post, both in English, both published from **Admin → Blog → ＋ Article** and
then shared to Facebook. The admin panel checks them before they go live.

---

## 1. New film → review

Copy this, then fill it in with your own words.

```
Title:  <Film name> review — is it worth a ticket? (story, cast, rating)
Summary: <Film> (<year>, <language>, <genre>) reviewed: acting, direction and where you can watch it. Short, honest verdict inside.

⭐ Rating: 7.5 / 10

| Detail | |
| --- | --- |
| Director | |
| Cast | |
| Runtime | |
| Language | |
| Genre | |

### The short version (no spoilers)
Two sentences. What it feels like to sit through, and who it is for.

### The story (no spoilers)
Two or three paragraphs: the main character, the problem, the direction it travels.
Do **not** give away the ending.

> ⚠️ **Spoiler** — the section below reveals the ending
>
> ### What happens (spoilers)
> Write it here. The warning must come first.

### What works
- three to five points

### What does not
- two to four points (an honest review needs these)

### Who should watch it
- ✅ People who like <genre>
- ❌ Not for <reason>

### Where to watch it (legal places only)
- Cinema: (while it is showing)
- Netflix / Prime Video / Dialog ViU / Peo TV — if it is there
- ❌ Never link a torrent, a Telegram channel or a "watch free" site.

Tags: film review, <film name>, <genre>, sri lanka
```

**Panel checks you will see:**
- `copied-synopsis` — a stock phrase ("the film follows…", "things take a turn…") means the
  summary was copied from another site. Write it yourself.
- `pirate-link` — an **error**: a torrent / Telegram / rip host is linked. Nothing publishes
  with it in the page.
- `spoiler-not-marked` — the ending is revealed before any "Spoiler" warning.
- `no-rating`, `no-where-to-watch` — nudges: a score and a legal "where to watch" line are
  what make a review searchable.

## 2. Actor news / rumours → short post

```
Title:  <Name> — what is actually confirmed (and what is only a rumour)
Summary: <Name> news: what has been confirmed by <source>, and what is still only talk.

### What is confirmed
- One line per fact, each with the source (interview, official page, film studio).

### Still only a rumour
- Say plainly: "Rumour — not confirmed."
- Write "According to <media outlet>" — never present gossip as fact.

### Why it matters
Two or three sentences of your own view.

Tags: <name>, film news, sri lanka
```

**Rules the panel and the site keep:**
1. **A named source for every fact.** "Reports say" with no name is not a source.
2. **Never state a rumour as fact.** Write "rumour" or "unconfirmed" in the sentence itself.
3. **No private information.** Home addresses, phone numbers, medical details, leaked photos
   and private messages are off limits — for a public figure too. That is not journalism,
   and it is what gets a page taken down (and a Facebook page reported).
4. **No court/legal claims** unless the case is public record, and then say "charged with",
   never "guilty of".
5. **Photos:** only ones you took or ones the owner allows. Otherwise publish the post
   without a picture.

## Publishing + Facebook

1. Save the article in the panel. A blog post is added to `sitemap.xml` and announced to
   Google and Bing automatically — nothing else to click.
2. For Facebook, write a **short English caption** (2–3 lines) and paste the article link.
   The site uses the article title, summary and image for the preview card, so the summary
   line matters: it is what people read before they tap.
3. Do not paste the whole article into Facebook — the link preview brings people to the page
   where the reading (and the ad) happens.
